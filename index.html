The solution

QuantisNexis replaces static evaluation models with a dynamic, data-driven intelligence system.

One platform. Four intelligence layers. Purpose-built for institutions that require defensible, auditable, and sector-calibrated organizational intelligence.

  • Quantitative scoring engine — 25 normalized variables, five intelligence pillars, peer-reviewed mathematical framework
  • Risk signal detection — QuantisSentra™ decay engine with per-sector thresholds and trend analysis
  • Simulation and uncertainty modeling — Monte Carlo analysis with 95% credible intervals on every score
  • AI-assisted interpretation — Quantis Narrator™ generates score-safe executive summaries and board briefs

QuantisSentra™

Early warning. Before the score deteriorates.

QuantisSentra™ detects institutional decay before it appears in the Quantis Index. Five decay dimensions monitored with per-sector thresholds calibrated by mission-criticality.

GREEN — Stable
DR < 0.20–0.25
No urgent intervention required. Sustain monitoring cadence and quarterly review.
AMBER — Elevated
DR 0.20–0.42
Proactive management response recommended within 30 days.
RED — High Risk
DR 0.35–0.60
Senior leadership action required within 14 days. Board briefing warranted.
CRITICAL — Immediate
DR ≥ 0.50–0.60
Executive intervention required immediately. Board escalation mandatory.
