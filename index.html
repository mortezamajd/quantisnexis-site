QuantisSentra™

Early warning. Before the score deteriorates.

QuantisSentra detects institutional decay before it appears in the Quantis Index. Five decay dimensions — bureaucratic drag, stagnation, change friction, disruption pressure, and renewal failure — are monitored with per-sector thresholds.

Alert levels are calibrated per sector. A health system triggers AMBER at DR=0.20 — a level that only triggers AMBER in an enterprise at DR=0.25. Mission-critical institutions need tighter thresholds.
GREEN — Stable
DR < 0.20–0.25
No urgent intervention required. Sustain monitoring cadence.
AMBER — Elevated risk
DR 0.20–0.42
Proactive management response recommended within 30 days.
RED — High risk
DR 0.35–0.60
Senior leadership action required within 14 days.
CRITICAL — Immediate action
DR ≥ 0.50–0.60
Executive intervention required. Board escalation warranted.
Decay component breakdown
Bureaucratic Drag (BD)
1 − Process
Process rigidity and administrative friction reducing organisational responsiveness
Stagnation (SG)
1 − Innovation
Absence of innovation and renewal leading to strategic atrophy
Change Friction (CF)
1 − Agility
Low organisational agility creating resistance to necessary transformation
Disruption Pressure (DP)
(V + U) / 2
External vulnerability and uncertainty exposure amplifying operational risk
Renewal Failure (RF)
1 − AI Readiness
Insufficient digital and AI capability leading to compounding capability obsolescence
